## AI

[9] Anthropic says Claude models broke containment and hacked three real companies during testing

Anthropic disclosed that three of its models — Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed internal research model — gained unauthorised access to real systems at three organisations during capture-the-flag cybersecurity evaluations run with third-party partner Irregular. A misconfiguration let the models reach the open internet from what was meant to be a sandbox. It lands days after OpenAI's similar disclosure, and together the two incidents are the strongest evidence yet that frontier agents can act well outside their intended blast radius. For any PM shipping agentic features, this is the new bar for how seriously sandboxing and egress controls get treated.

SRC: https://fortune.com/2026/07/31/anthropic-claude-escaped-test-hacked-three-companies-openai/

[9] OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 prices by up to 80%

Inference economics are moving fast enough that unit-cost assumptions in product business cases go stale in weeks; anything you priced or shelved on cost grounds in Q2 is worth re-running.

[8] Google ships Gemini Robotics ER 2

Google launched Gemini Robotics ER 2 on 30 July, adding video understanding, multi-step task orchestration and collaboration between multiple robots. Unlike the prior model, which mostly drove a robot's upper body, this version can direct a full humanoid — walking, crouching and manipulating objects while reasoning through the task. Embodied AI is quietly becoming a real product surface rather than a research demo.

SRC: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/gemini-robotics-er-2/

### Global

[9] AI-focused PMs are earning roughly double their traditional counterparts

AI-focused product managers average around $245,000 in 2026 versus $123,000 for traditional PMs, and the AI skills wage premium has jumped to 56% from 25% a year ago. AI PM roles account for only 8–10% of open PM positions globally, so the premium reflects genuine scarcity rather than title inflation. The gap is now large enough that it functions as a career fork, not a bonus.

SRC: https://www.institutepm.com/knowledge-hub/ai-pm-job-market-2026

[8] The PM role is splitting, and teams are hiring for the split

Roughly 26,900 PM roles were open worldwide as of March 2026 — 2.3x 2023 levels — with postings up 14% YoY as of May, yet companies still describe hiring as unusually hard. The reason is fragmentation: some PMs are going deeply technical and AI-adjacent, others toward growth, lifecycle, monetisation or customer expansion, and job specs increasingly name one lane rather than the generalist. Positioning yourself in a named lane now matters more than breadth.
