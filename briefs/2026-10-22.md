## AI
[9] Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive agent for professionals and small businesses
Claude Duet is a new app where Claude works as an agent. It surfaces what needs attention, completes tasks, keeps working when you step away, and checks with you before acting. It can build dashboards, schedule meetings and draft forecasts, and it can buy business supplies through Stripe's Link wallet. It was designed around Claude Opus 5.5. It is a waitlist research preview for US Pro and Max subscribers, and it answers Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots. For PMs it shows the agent race moving from chat to always-on delegation with approval gates.
