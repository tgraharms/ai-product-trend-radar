## AI
[9] Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive always-on agent
Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet. It is an agent that sends daily briefs, watches your connected apps, and builds dashboards, schedules meetings and drafts forecasts. It runs on its own virtual machine and keeps working when your computer is off. Sending messages, payments, signing and deletions still need your approval. ## COMMUNITY
[8] AI-built open-source clones of Microsoft Office and Adobe apps keep multiplying
Developers are using coding agents to rebuild paid software for free. The recent wave includes ElixirOffice (AGPL, LLM-written), Ely Office and BetterOffice, following ArtCraft's Adobe-style suite built with Claude. Behind it is REA (Reverse Engineer Anything), an MIT-licensed tool that lets agents inspect compiled apps and rebuild features. It has drawn hundreds of thousands of GitHub stars, and Perplexity has added it to its agent. No company response has been reported yet, though cease-and-desist letters are widely expected. It matters because the moat of closed, expensive software is eroding fast, and PMs at incumbents will feel it in pricing and differentiation.
SRC: https://itsfoss.com/newsletter/local-ai-weekly-6/

[6] Microsoft keeps retreating on Copilot in Windows 11 after "Microslop" backlash

It is also bringing back the taskbar Ask feature without the Copilot name. Microsoft calls it simplifying. It is a clear case of community pushback reshaping a big-tech AI rollout.
