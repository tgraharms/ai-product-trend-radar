## AI

[9] EU AI Act enforcement is now live — transparency rules in force

On 2 August the European Commission's AI Office formally began enforcing the AI Act, including new transparency obligations: chatbots must identify themselves as AI, deepfakes must be labelled, and AI-generated content needs machine-readable marks. Fines can reach €15 million or 3% of global turnover; high-risk system rules were pushed to December 2027. Any PM shipping AI features touching EU users should check compliance now.
