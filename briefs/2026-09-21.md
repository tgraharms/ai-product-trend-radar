### Global

[8] The PM role is splitting — and pay is splitting with it

Analysis of 2026 trends describes the generalist PM dividing into "Builder-PMs" (AI-native, prototype in code, work on smaller PM-to-engineer ratios) and "Integrator-PMs" (high-EQ, cross-functional, roadmap owners drifting toward product marketing). The compensation gap is stark as reported: AI-focused PMs around USD 245k versus about USD 123k for traditional PMs. With AI producing polished applications, employers are also leaning on proof-of-work — published writing, teardowns, recorded talks — and warm intros over résumé signals. The practical takeaway: pick a sharp specialization and make your product judgment visible.

Tools and platforms are increasingly tagging AI-made images, video and audio using provenance standards like C2PA / Content Credentials and Google's SynthID, and 2026 is bringing new watermarking expectations for AI video in several markets.
