## AI

[9] Amodei, Altman and Musk publicly call to slow frontier AI

Over the weekend of 12–14 September, Anthropic's Dario Amodei argued AI labs "must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models," citing loss-of-control, cyber/bio misuse and economic-disruption risks; Sam Altman and Elon Musk both agreed publicly. Altman clarified "pacing" doesn't mean "stopping," and notably no company announced any actual change to its roadmap — so read this as a positioning move as much as a safety one. It's the clearest joint signal yet that the frontier labs want to be seen managing risk, which matters for how AI features get shipped and governed.

[7] Proof-of-work now beats the résumé in PM hiring

Despite 42,000+ open PM roles, landing one has gotten harder because AI-generated applications drown out genuine signal — so hiring is shifting toward published writing, recorded talks, detailed teardowns, demonstrated decision frameworks and warm intros over job boards, with employers leaning on structured work samples and non-prepped interviews. The practical takeaway: a small portfolio of public work is now the highest-leverage career investment for a PM. Business acumen and go-to-market fluency are the skills most cited as mattering next.
