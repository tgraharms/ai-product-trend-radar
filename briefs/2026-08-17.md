## AI

[9] Anthropic explains how Claude's text watermark works

Anthropic published a detailed explainer on 14 August confirming that future Claude models will embed a statistical watermark in generated text, using a version of Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text method. It says the watermark costs nothing extra, is invisible to readers, carries no user-identifying data, and is being added to comply with the EU AI Act. A detection API is coming, and generated image files will carry C2PA content credentials instead. For PMs shipping AI-written content, this changes the provenance conversation: "was this AI-assisted" becomes a checkable question rather than a guess.

[8] EU AI Act transparency rules are now live, forcing AI content labelling

Article 50 transparency obligations took effect on 2 August 2026, with limited deferrals to 2 December for generative systems already on the market. Around 190 signatories, including the major labs, signed the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content in July. The Code favours a layered approach: metadata embedding, imperceptible watermarking, and logging. If your product generates text, image, audio or video for EU users, labelling is now a compliance requirement, not a nice-to-have.

SRC: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/

### Malaysia

[7] Malaysia's data centre boom is huge in ringgit, thin in direct headcount

Bernama reports 143 data centre investment projects approved between 2021 and 30 June 2025, worth RM144.4 billion, but creating only 1,429 new jobs directly. Penang holds the semiconductor and high-value electronics base while Johor has become the data centre cluster absorbing Singapore's spillover. Budget 2026 allocates RM1.36 billion to the Ministry of Digital, including RM1.11 billion for development. For a PM here, the honest read is that the value capture is in the software and AI layer sitting on top of that infrastructure, not in the facilities themselves.
