[9] Anthropic to put 5% of its shares into a new public-benefit fund

Anthropic says it will commit 5% of company stock (subject to shareholder approval) to an "Anthropic Public Benefit Fund," with another 2% added for every 50% rise in company value. The money goes to nonprofits and direct payouts to people, with AI-driven job disruption named as a focus; it can't be used for lobbying or political donations. For a company heading toward an IPO at a reported $2T+ valuation, it's a notable move to bake public-benefit commitments into the cap table.

[7] Google open-sources Harness², an agent that rewrites its own tooling

Google Research released Harness², an open-source project where an agent adjusts its own prompts, tools, and memory for each task while the underlying model stays fixed. It's part of a broader shift toward getting more out of existing models through better scaffolding rather than only bigger models — a useful pattern for product teams working within fixed model budgets.
