[7] 2026 is shaping up as Malaysia's worst year for job losses since 2020 — and half are white-collar

By mid-June, 42,807 workers had registered losses with PERKESO, running about 36% above the same period in 2025, concentrated in KL and Selangor. The takeaway for Malaysian PMs and tech professionals: the risk is real enough to keep skills sharp, even if the "AI did it" verdict is still "not yet."
