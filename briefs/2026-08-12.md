## AI

[9] Anthropic makes Claude Code "auto mode" the default from 14 August

From Friday, new Claude Code sessions on Pro, Max and Team plans default to auto mode, where Claude executes without routine permission prompts and a separate classifier model screens each action before it runs. Anthropic cites a controlled study in which humans caught planted dangerous commands 13.6% of the time versus 89% for the classifier. Enterprise, API and cloud users follow within a month. For PMs, this is a live case study in shipping a default-on autonomy change and using a model as the safety layer rather than the user.

[9] OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber and splits Daybreak into Red and Blue tiers

Access is gated behind identity verification, monitoring and legal attestations — a notable product pattern of shipping capability behind eligibility rather than behind safety filters.

[8] Meta open-sources Muse Glimmer, a 30B agentic model that runs on one consumer GPU

Meta claims it leads Gemma4-31B and Qwen3.6-27B on agentic orchestration benchmarks. Local, licence-free agent inference changes the build-versus-buy maths for anyone costing out agent features.

[7] EU AI Act transparency obligations are now being enforced

Since 2 August the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities have been enforcing the AI Act's transparency rules: chatbots must disclose they are AI, and AI-generated or altered content must be labelled and machine-readable. Noncompliance carries fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover. Note that full compliance for standalone Annex III high-risk systems — recruitment, credit scoring, education — has slipped to 2 December 2027, so the near-term work is disclosure and provenance, not high-risk conformity.
