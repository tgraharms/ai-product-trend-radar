The delay buys compliance breathing room but keeps the regulatory direction intact — relevant for anyone shipping AI features into EU markets.

## TECH

[9] OpenAI and Broadcom unveil "Jalapeño," a custom LLM-inference chip

OpenAI revealed its first custom accelerator, co-developed with Broadcom and taped out in roughly nine months — one of the fastest advanced-ASIC cycles on record, partly sped up using OpenAI's own models. Engineering samples are already running workloads including GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark, with performance-per-watt well above current state of the art and first deployment targeted for end of 2026. It is a clear move to own the full inference stack and cut dependence on Nvidia.

Full rollout is expected by end of summer. The reversal of Apple's "Apple-silicon only" stance underscores how hard frontier AI is to self-host at scale.
