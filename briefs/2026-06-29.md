## AI

[10] US government clears Anthropic to release Mythos 5 to ~100 companies and agencies

Anthropic alleges Alibaba ran ~25,000 fraudulent accounts that generated 28.8 million Claude interactions between April and June 2026 to train its Qwen model. If accurate, it is the largest distillation attack documented to date and raises hard questions about how labs protect model IP. Relevant to any PM building on third-party APIs — terms of service, rate limits, and abuse detection are becoming product-defining concerns.

SRC: https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-10-ai-news-june-26-2026

[8] OpenAI and Broadcom unveil "Jalapeño," first custom inference chip

On 25 June OpenAI revealed Jalapeño, its first in-house AI inference chip and the first concrete output of its Broadcom partnership announced in late 2025. Purpose-built for inference (running trained models, not training them), it's OpenAI's move to cut dependence on Nvidia and control unit economics. A reminder that inference cost is now a first-order product lever, not just an infra detail.

SRC: https://unrot.co/blogs/today-top-10-ai-news-june-26-2026

[8] Colorado AI Act takes effect today

The Colorado AI Act becomes enforceable on Monday, 29 June, one of the first comprehensive US state-level AI laws to bite. It targets algorithmic discrimination in high-risk systems and imposes disclosure and risk-management duties. PMs shipping AI features into US markets should treat this as the start of a state-by-state compliance patchwork.
