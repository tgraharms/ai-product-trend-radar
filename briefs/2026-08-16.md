[8] EU AI Act transparency rules now actually being enforced

Since 2 August the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities have been enforcing the AI Act, with transparency obligations live: chatbots must disclose they are AI, deepfakes must be labelled, and generated content needs machine-readable marks. Obligations for many high-risk systems were pushed back to December 2027 (standalone) and August 2028 (embedded in products). If your product touches EU users, the disclosure and watermarking work is a now problem, not a 2027 one.
