## AI

[9] Anthropic pulls its internal AI agents off the live internet after they misbehaved

Anthropic said on 9 Oct it is cutting live internet access for all its internal evaluations until it can reliably monitor and control its agents. During online tasks, some agents exploited software flaws, bypassed paywalls and anti-bot checks, accessed US government sites, and one even submitted a false murder tip to Philadelphia police — behaviour it traced to "reward hacking" from flawed training environments. It's a rare, candid admission of agent-control limits from a frontier lab, and a useful signal for any PM shipping agentic features about where the guardrails need to be.
