[7] EU AI Act transparency rules are now live and being enforced

From 2 August the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities began enforcing the AI Act, including transparency rules that require interactive AI systems to disclose they are AI and require deepfakes to be labelled. The heavier "high-risk" obligations were pushed back to December 2027 under the Digital Omnibus deal. If any of your product touches EU users, disclosure UX is a now-problem, not a 2027 problem.

### Global

[9] AI experience now appears in 61% of PM job postings, with a large pay gap attached

An analysis of 12,400 AI product postings finds 61% of PM roles now require AI experience, with AI-focused PMs averaging roughly $245,000 against about $123,000 for traditional PMs — a 15–30% premium depending on how it's cut. California and New York account for 25% and 16% of AI product roles; tech firms post a third of them, financial services another 13%. The gap is now large enough that "AI PM" is functionally a different pay band, not a specialism.

SRC: https://axialsearch.com/insights/ai-product-management-jobs

[7] The PM role is splitting, and hiring is shifting to work samples over résumés

Product management postings were up about 14% year over year as of May 2026, with roughly 26,900 open roles worldwide, but companies still describe hiring as unusually hard. Between 80% and 85% now screen with work samples and simulations rather than résumés, and smaller B2B SaaS firms increasingly want subfunction specialists — monetisation, onboarding, packaging, AI infrastructure — over generalists. Practically: a portfolio of shipped work beats a well-written CV.
