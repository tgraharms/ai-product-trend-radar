[8] EU AI Act transparency rules for general-purpose AI and synthetic media hit hard enforcement on 2 August

As of 2 August 2026, businesses must tell users when they are talking to an AI chatbot or looking at AI-generated content, with penalties up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover. High-risk system deadlines were pushed out (Annex III to December 2027, Annex I to August 2028), so the near-term compliance burden is disclosure and labelling, not full conformity assessment. California's AI Transparency Act also became operative the same day. For a PM, this is a concrete product requirement: AI disclosure in the UI, not a legal-team problem.

### Malaysia

[7] Malaysia's AI talent gap is the structural story: 30,000 professionals targeted by 2030, roughly 3,000 today

The National AI Action Plan targets 30,000 AI professionals by 2030 against a current base of about 3,000, while TalentCorp warns up to 697,000 Malaysian jobs could be significantly affected by AI, digitalisation and the green economy within three to five years without upskilling. TalentCorp and EY launched the RM110 million Jelajah AI MyMahir upskilling drive in January 2026, and Malaysia is aiming for 100,000 AI-literate workers with structured credentials by Q4 2026. AI job postings in Malaysia are up 38% year-on-year since 2023. For a PM here, the scarce-skill premium is real and the credential infrastructure is being built right now.
