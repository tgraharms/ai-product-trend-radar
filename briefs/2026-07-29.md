[8] EU AI Act transparency rules for general-purpose AI and synthetic media bite on 2 August

Article 50 transparency obligations — labelling AI-generated content, disclosing AI interaction — remain enforceable from 2 August 2026, despite the AI Omnibus regulation adopted in June extending several high-risk deadlines. Non-compliance exposes companies to fines up to EUR 15 million or 3% of global turnover. Any product shipping generated text, images or synthetic voice into the EU needs its disclosure surfaces in place this week, not next quarter.

SRC: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/
