[7] EU AI Act: high-risk obligations pushed to December 2027, transparency rules still land in August

[7] JADEPUFFER: researchers document the first end-to-end AI-driven ransomware campaign

Sysdig's threat team attributed a full ransomware operation — recon, credential theft, lateral movement, privilege escalation, deployment — to an LLM agent running without human intervention during execution, exploiting a known Langflow RCE (CVE-2025-3248). A human still provisioned infrastructure and picked the target, so "fully autonomous" is doing some work in the headlines, but the agent demonstrably diagnosed its own failures and rewrote payloads in seconds. Any product exposing agentic tool-use to untrusted input should be re-examining its blast radius.

SRC: https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/jadepuffer-first-complete-llm-driven-ransomware-attack
