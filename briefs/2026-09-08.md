[8] AI watermarking goes mainstream — and gets messy

Google now lets users strip the visible watermark from its AI-generated images, video, and songs, though the invisible SynthID mark and C2PA metadata stay put. The catch for everyday users: a watermark hit is a signal, not proof — Google and Anthropic both warn nobody should lose a job or a grade over a detection score alone.
