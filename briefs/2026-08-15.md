### Malaysia

Malaysian retrenchment volumes have climbed through 2026, and high-skilled workers made up the largest affected group in the first five months of the year — continuing a pattern where they accounted for over half of retrenchments from 2021 to 2025. The open policy question is whether Malaysia simply is not creating enough high-skilled roles, or whether AI is displacing them. Either way, positioning matters: PM roles tied to measurable revenue or cost outcomes are safer than roles defined by coordination.

[7] The PM role is splitting into specialisations

Analysis of 2026 hiring points to a market moving from generalist "product manager" searches toward specific ones — AI PM, API PM, consumer PM — with generalists seeing slower progression when they try to job-hop. Hiring processes are also shifting to structured work samples and interviews that cannot be prepped with a language model, plus more warm-channel sourcing over job boards. The implication is to pick a lane and build proof in it, and to keep a live network rather than relying on applications.
