## AI

[9] Anthropic launches Claude Science, an AI workbench for researchers

Anthropic unveiled Claude Science in beta on June 30, pulling a researcher's scattered tools, data packages, and compute access into one workbench where AI agents can run large chunks of the work and produce auditable results. Anthropic is also funding up to 50 research projects with up to $30,000 in credits each (applications open through July 15). It's Anthropic's biggest push yet into scientific R&D and a signal of where it sees the next enterprise wedge for Claude.

SRC: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench

[8] RAISE US launches with $500M+ to retrain workers for the AI economy, Anthropic among anchor funders

Former Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo and ex-Governor Eric Holcomb launched RAISE US on June 25, a nonpartisan nonprofit targeting $1B in commitments to retrain American workers displaced by AI. Anthropic, Amazon, Microsoft, and the OpenAI Foundation are anchor partners, with pilot states including Arkansas, Connecticut, Maryland, and Utah. Raimondo's line — "America has a technology strategy... it does not yet have a people strategy" — is a useful frame for any PM shipping AI features that touch jobs.

SRC: https://www.axios.com/2026/06/25/anthropic-labor-market-ai-jobs-crisis

Co-founder Sergey Brin's internal memo called for urgently closing the "agentic execution" gap, after Google's own CFO admitted Anthropic codes close to 100% of its work with AI versus roughly 50% at Google — a stark marker of Anthropic's lead in developer tooling.

[7] GitHub Copilot's usage-based billing triggers 10x–50x bill spikes for power users

The first full billing cycle under Copilot's new usage-based pricing (effective June 1) closed June 30, with developers reporting projected monthly costs jumping from $29 to $750 or more for heavy agentic workflows. GitHub's CPO acknowledged "Copilot is not the same product it was a year ago." For PMs evaluating AI coding tools, this accelerates the case for flat-rate alternatives like Claude Code, and previews the same pricing reckoning coming to other agentic AI products.

SRC: https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-copilot-is-moving-to-usage-based-billing/

[6] EU Parliament approves delay of key AI Act high-risk obligations

On June 16 the European Parliament granted final approval to amendments delaying application of significant high-risk AI system obligations under the EU AI Act — standalone high-risk systems (employment, credit scoring, critical infrastructure, etc.) now face an August 2026 deadline pushed to December 2027. Product teams selling into the EU get more runway on compliance, but the goalposts are still moving.
